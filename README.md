# Pro League of Legends Pick/Ban Tracker

**Live app:** [paste your Streamlit link]

## Who it's for
Fans, amateur teams and aspiring analysts who follow [LCK] and want a quick view of what pro teams pick and ban each patch.

## Question it answers
How do pick and ban rates in pro play change from patch to patch?

## Data
Match data from Oracle's Elixir. This is a fan project and is not endorsed by Riot Games.

## Status
v0.1: descriptive pick and ban rates by patch. See ROADMAP.md and CHANGELOG.md.

## Known limitations
Pro leagues play relatively few games per patch, so rates from small samples can swing a lot.
